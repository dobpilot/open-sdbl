# Design

ConfigHeader gains `defined_type: ConfigTypeIdentity`. The measured class-0 five-element owner wrapper is recognized by one helper shared with the full decoder. The own header must match the resource. Unknown wrappers remain Unknown; a measured nil GUID is Absent. Existing generated identities keep their current interpretation.

The native UNF basis resource and its independent XML-derived type identity provide the positive oracle. Mutated wrapper and nil cases are synthetic negative/absence controls, not new native measurements.

Resolution retains unknown references when their definition has not been acquired. Known definitions with invalid or cyclic declarations remain fail-closed. The native Event partial-acquisition control proves that both `reference_types` and `declared_types` retain the missing defined-type GUID after snapshot resolution; acquiring Basis expands the declaration to 56 members. The resolver also preserves missing definitions through its expansion fallback. No additional raw dependency API is required or introduced.
